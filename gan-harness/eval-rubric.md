# Design Evaluation Rubric

This rubric evaluates the aesthetic excellence, textured feel, and overall premium polish of the redesigned landing page.

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Visual Textures & Background (35 points)
* **Score 30-35**: Stunning visual depth achieved through noise textures, grid backdrops, and seamless gradient masks. No large flat-colored regions.
* **Score 20-29**: Grid patterns are present, but noise/gradients feel static or lack responsive masking.
* **Score 0-19**: Flat backgrounds, plain colors, or uninspired defaults.

### 2. Glowing Borders & Bezel Detail (35 points)
* **Score 30-35**: Cards and mockups use fine high-fidelity double borders, custom glowing borders, soft radial shadow drop-offs, and custom glowing elements.
* **Score 20-29**: Basic hover shadows and single border rules are present but don't feel luxury/award-winning.
* **Score 0-19**: Solid borders with no drop shadow, glow, or lighting effects.

### 3. Layout, Typography & Harmony (30 points)
* **Score 25-30**: Premium typography hierarchy. Excellent contrast, fluid layout transitions, clean spacings, and elegant text animations.
* **Score 15-24**: Typography is legible but uses generic weights/colors. Alignment lacks precise grid coherence.
* **Score 0-14**: Messy spacing, poor layout alignment, or default styling.
